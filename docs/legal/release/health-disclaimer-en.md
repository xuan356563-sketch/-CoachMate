## Important Notice: Please Read Carefully

**CoachMate ("the App") is a training assistance tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescriptions.**

By using the App, you understand and agree to the following:

### 1. Not Medical Advice

- All content provided by the App — including but not limited to training plans, exercise guidance, recovery suggestions, body metric analysis, AI coaching suggestions, weekly reports, and race preparation advice — is **for reference only** and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- The App does not replace evaluation and advice from a doctor, physical therapist, sports medicine specialist, or other qualified medical professional.

### 2. Your Responsibility

- Before starting a new training program or significantly increasing training intensity, especially if any of the following applies, consult a qualified medical professional first:
  - known cardiovascular disease, hypertension, diabetes, or other chronic conditions;
  - pregnancy or postpartum recovery;
  - previous surgery, fracture, joint or muscle injury;
  - a long period without regular exercise;
  - older age or family health risks.
- If you experience chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, extreme fatigue, or other discomfort during exercise, stop immediately and seek medical help.

### 3. Pain and Rehab Logs

- The App's pain and rehab logging features are for **recording and reminding only**, not diagnosis.
- If pain persists for more than 2 weeks, reaches 7/10 or above, or is accompanied by numbness, weakness, swelling, or other abnormal symptoms, the App will prompt you to stop related training and **see a doctor**. Please follow this guidance.
- Rehab-related suggestions do not constitute a rehab treatment prescription.

### 4. Emergencies

- In a medical emergency (e.g., serious injury, chest pain, fainting), call emergency services immediately (120 in mainland China) or go to the nearest medical facility. Do not rely on the App for emergencies.

### 5. Limitations of AI Content

- AI-generated suggestions may contain errors or may not account for your specific situation. AI suggestions are **for your reference only**; you decide whether to adopt them.
- All AI results in the App are shown as confirm-before-apply cards; they only affect training after your confirmation.

### 6. Third-Party and External Content

- Books, knowledge base entries, and external materials referenced in the App are for learning reference only and do not represent medical consensus.

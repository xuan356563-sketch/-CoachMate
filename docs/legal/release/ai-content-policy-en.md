## 1. AI Features Overview

The App provides the following AI features ("AI Services"):

| Feature | Capability | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI Trainer | Training Q&A, plan generation | Text or transcribed speech you confirmed, de-identified training summary | Training suggestion cards |
| AI Motion | Exercise photo scoring and form reference | Exercise photos you confirmed | Structured movement quality analysis |
| AI Recovery | Recovery suggestions | De-identified sleep/resting HR/HRV/fatigue summary you consented to | Recovery suggestion cards |

## 2. AI Content Labeling

- All AI-generated content (text cards, image analysis results) is clearly labeled **"AI-generated" or "AI suggestion"** and distinguished from your own input.
- AI results are shown as confirm-before-apply cards; **they only affect local training after your confirmation**.

## 3. Content Safety Mechanisms

- The server performs **structured validation** of AI inputs and outputs; only schema-validated artifacts are persisted.
- Local safety rules (pain red lines, rehab constraints, rule engine audits) **always take precedence over AI suggestions**; AI suggestions cannot override safety red lines.
- AI prompts are security-audited; health and safety outputs are re-checked by local rules.

## 4. User Reporting Channel

If you find AI-generated content with any of the following issues, please report it via the channels below:

- unlawful or violating content (pornography, violence, fraud, etc.);
- infringing content (plagiarism, copyright or portrait rights violations, etc.);
- inappropriate health/medical advice;
- any other content you believe is inappropriate.

### How to Report

1. **In-app**: mark an AI suggestion card as "inaccurate" or "report" (where available).
2. **Email**: send an email to [Contact Email] with the subject "AI Content Report", including:
   - the time of the report;
   - a screenshot or description of the AI card;
   - why you believe it is inappropriate.

### Our Commitment

- We will review reported content within **7 business days**;
- For confirmed violations, we will **remove the content, roll back its impact on training**, and correct related model output;
- We reserve the right to take action against abuse of the reporting channel.

## 5. Limitations of AI Services

- AI content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated; it is for your reference only, and you decide whether to adopt it.
- The App's AI does not provide medical diagnosis or prescriptions and does not replace professional advice.
- For health and safety decisions, please follow the App's safety red lines and the Health Disclaimer.

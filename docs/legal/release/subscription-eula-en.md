- The subscription period is **monthly auto-renewing**. The price is as shown on the in-app purchase page and the App Store / Google Play.

- The subscription is auto-renewing. Unless you cancel at least **24 hours** before the end of the current period in your App Store / Google Play subscription settings, the subscription will renew and be charged automatically.
- After cancellation, you may continue using the Membership until the end of the current paid period.

- If a free trial is offered on the purchase page, no charge applies during the trial. Canceling before the trial ends incurs no charge.
